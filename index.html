02

Timing & season

Departure is pencilled for Tuesday 23 March 2027, the start of the Irish post-primary Easter break, to keep missed school to the week after Easter.

Late March to mid-April is autumn in Australia. Queensland is warm and settled (mid-20s°C, water still 24–25°C — ideal for the foiling and first surf lessons); Sydney is mild with warm ocean; Melbourne / Mornington is cooler and changeable (bring layers for the coast walks); Perth is dry and mild. Autumn also means smaller, cleaner surf — good for beginners. Easter Sunday is 28 March, mid-way through the Noosa leg, so book activities there early.

Animals in the wild

Wild sightings only where we can — no cages. Note: wild koalas are an east-coast animal (Queensland, NSW, Victoria), so Noosa is the best koala shot; there are none in the wild around Perth.

Singapore

Otters · water monitors · long-tailed macaques

Wild smooth-coated otter families around Marina Bay and Kallang at dawn/dusk; monitors and macaques on the MacRitchie boardwalk.

Noosa

Koalas · dolphins · bush turkeys · lorikeets

Wild koalas in the gums along the Noosa National Park coastal track (Tea Tree Bay stretch); bottlenose dolphins off the headland; rainbow lorikeets everywhere.

Sydney — Freshwater / Manly

Blue gropers · little penguins · water dragons

Blue gropers snorkelling Cabbage Tree Bay; a protected little-penguin colony breeds around Manly's North Harbour; eastern water dragons on the coast track.

Blue Mountains

Wombats · swamp wallabies · lyrebirds · cockatoos

Wombats and grey kangaroos in the Megalong Valley at dusk; superb lyrebirds scratching beside the Leura tracks; sulphur-crested and gang-gang cockatoos.

Mornington Peninsula

Kangaroos · echidnas · seals · seadragons · dolphins

Grey kangaroos at dawn in Greens Bush; echidnas on the Cape Schanck tracks; weedy seadragons, fur seals and dolphins on a bay kayak.

Perth & Rottnest

Quokkas · sea lions · wild dolphins · NZ fur seals

Quokkas all over car-free Rottnest; Australian sea lions, little penguins and wild dolphins on a Penguin Island sea-kayak trip; ospreys over Cottesloe.

02

Timing & season

Departure is Friday 19 March 2027 and you land home on Saturday 17 April. Easter falls across the middle: Good Friday 26 Mar in Margaret River, Easter Saturday 27 Mar on Rottnest (busy, so book the ferry), Easter Sunday 28 Mar for the flight to Melbourne, and Easter Monday 29 Mar at Mornington — so expect some closures on the Sunday.

Late March in the South West of WA is late-summer/early-autumn — warm days, water still swimmable at Yallingup, caves and forests at their best. The Mornington Peninsula is cooling into autumn — bring layers and a rain jacket for the coast. Sydney in the first half of April is mild and settled, the best stretch of the year for the Northern Beaches ocean pools.

No early starts: nothing leaving before about 8am. That rules out the cheapest Singapore→Perth flight (3:15am) and a Perth→Melbourne red-eye, so the prices in this option use later flights, which costs about €100 more in total.

Budget — to be filled in

Gulf hubs are the live risk. The Iran conflict is flaring again: EASA has told carriers to avoid Gulf airspace (UAE, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain, Kuwait) through at least 16 Nov 2026, and Emirates, Etihad and Qatar are on reduced schedules (AirHelp, 30 Sep).

Insurers treat the conflict as a known event — AA Ireland's cut-off is policies bought from 28 Feb 2026 (AA Ireland) — so a policy bought now won't pay out if you cancel over it. If you do fly via the Gulf, fare flexibility, not insurance, is what protects you.

The outbound is not a Gulf problem: the Turkish and Lufthansa-group flights above are 1-stop through Europe/Turkey and cost about the same as the Gulf options. The Gulf only comes in on the way home — Sydney to Dublin has no 1-stop alternative; Gulf-free routings need two stops and cost €3,400 or more.

Jet lag: the outbound options leave Dublin late afternoon or evening and land in Singapore around 4–7pm the next day, so you sleep on the plane and arrive in time to stay up until bedtime. Perth is the same time zone as Singapore. Melbourne and Sydney are 3 hours ahead of Perth. Going home is westbound and kinder; the Qatar flights leave Sydney mid-afternoon or at 8:55pm and fly through the night.

Nut allergy protocol

Severe nut allergy, EpiPen carrier. Applies to whichever option we land on — this shapes airline, city and dining choices, it is not a footnote.

  • EpiPens (two, in date) carried in hand luggage at all times, split between two adults. GP letter for airport security.
  • Pre-flag the allergy on every flight booking — nut-free meal request, medical notes on the PNR, ask about buffer-zone seating and pre-boarding to wipe the area down.
  • Singapore is deliberate — high English fluency, clear packaged-food labelling, staff used to allergy questions.
  • Self-catering via HomeExchange helps — own kitchen for most stays cuts the restaurant risk right down.
  • Chocolate factories are a specific risk (Option B) — production lines are high cross-contamination territory. Call the Margaret River Chocolate Factory, and any cafe lunch you do book, ahead.
  • Carry an allergy translation / chef card; know the local emergency number (Singapore 995, Australia 000).
  • Travel insurance confirmed to cover anaphylaxis and medical evacuation; carry a copy of the policy.
